# Roadmap

## v1.0.0 (Current)
- Complete `src/`-layout PyPI package (`tyzombie` CLI)
- 8-level Campaign, Endless Survival, Daily Challenge, and Seeded Sandbox
- Mutators, Combo Fever, Achievements, Top-10 Leaderboard, Mid-Run Resume
- Cross-platform synthesized audio & 4 terminal color themes

## Future Ideas
- Custom user word-pack loader (`~/.tyzombie/packs/*.txt`)
- Local 2-player / split-barricade co-op mode
- Exportable replay timelines for personal-best runs
- Additional themed campaigns & boss mechanics
